The method

Diagnose Before You Prescribe

Most organizations prescribe training or automation for what is really a system problem. And when learning is the right answer, they measure attendance instead of capability. Five steps keep the order right.

Try the interactive RAPID scorer to see the Prescribe step in action.

  1. Examine

    Find the real complaint. Is it a skill gap or a system gap?

  2. Diagnose

    Map how the work actually flows. It usually breaks at a handoff or a manual step.

  3. Prescribe

    Decide what AI owns and what people keep. Then pick the tool.

  4. Treat

    Build it small, governed, and easy to adopt.

  5. Follow Up

    Build the learning, drive adoption, and measure the result. The step most teams skip.

Enterprise learning

Learning that shows up in business results

Technology changes nothing until people can use it well. From onboarding to director development, I design learning backward from the capability the strategy needs, deliver it in the modalities people actually use at work, including AI-enabled ones, and measure it on time to productivity, retention, and a ready bench, not completions.
