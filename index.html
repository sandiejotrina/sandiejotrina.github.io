The method

Diagnose Before You Prescribe

Most organizations prescribe training or automation for what is really a system problem. Five steps keep the order right.

Try the interactive RAPID scorer to see the Prescribe step in action.

  1. Examine

    Find the real complaint. Is it a skill gap or a system gap?

  2. Diagnose

    Map how the work actually flows. It usually breaks at a handoff or a manual step.

  3. Prescribe

    Decide what AI owns and what people keep. Then pick the tool.

  4. Treat

    Build it small, governed, and easy to adopt.

  5. Follow Up

    Drive adoption and measure the result. The step most teams skip.
