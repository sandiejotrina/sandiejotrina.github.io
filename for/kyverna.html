A working sample

A certification path for a cell therapy field team

One structure for every field role, with depth set by the role. A team member is certified when they can do the work, not when they finish the modules.

  1. Foundation. Disease state, unmet need, and the patient journey. Knowledge check.
  2. Product. Mechanism, manufacturing, and chain of identity from apheresis to infusion. Knowledge check.
  3. Safety. Monitoring and management of known risks, and how to respond to a site question. Scenario assessment.
  4. Compliance. Scientific exchange versus promotion, adverse event reporting, and documentation. Attestation on file.
  5. Field readiness. Observed role play and a supervised field visit. Certified by a manager, not by a quiz.
  6. Stay current. Recertify on new data, label changes, or protocol updates, not just on a calendar date.

The metric that matters: completion shows activity. Time to field readiness, certification pass rates by role, and the quality of site interactions show whether training is working.
